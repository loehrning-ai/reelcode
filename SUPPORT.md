# Support

reelcode is community-supported and published as-is. Issues may not get an answer, and nothing here promises a
release schedule or a fix for a particular problem.

- **Bugs:** open an issue with the command you ran, what you expected, what happened, your OS, `node --version`,
  `ffmpeg -version | head -1`, and the output of `npx reelcode doctor`. Reproduce with one of the `projects/example-*`
  projects when you can.
- **Questions and ideas:** open an issue labelled as a question or a feature request. Focused proposals with a use
  case are the most likely to land.
- **Security:** never in a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Do not attach footage, brand assets or recordings you are not allowed to share publicly.
